# 🌍 Global Health & Socio-Economic Indicators Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive, executive-level data visualization dashboard built in **Tableau Desktop**. Analyzing World Health Organization (WHO) datasets across 193 nations, the dashboard investigates key health outcomes, adult/child mortality rates, disease prevalence, and socio-economic drivers such as schooling and resource distribution.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Features

### 1. Life Expectancy & Human Development
* **Income Composition of Resources (HDI Proxy):** Identified as the strongest positive predictor of life expectancy ($\approx +0.72$ correlation), demonstrating how effective resource distribution directly correlates with increased longevity.
* **Development Gap:** Clear comparative metrics highlighting the average life expectancy variance between **Developed** (~79 years) and **Developing** (~67 years) nations.

### 2. Disease & Mortality Breakdown
* **Regional Mortality Trends:** Comprehensive analysis of adult mortality and under-5 mortality rates, surfacing regional disparities across continents.
* **Health & Lifestyle Indicators:** Interactive breakdown of BMI categories, schooling years, and alcohol consumption habits mapped against global health outcomes.

---

## 🛠️ Dashboard Design & UX Strategy
* **Design Theme:** Developed using a custom *"Modern Slate & Clean Blue"* palette with white floating card containers and faint gray borders for a polished corporate aesthetic.
* **Interactive Navigation:** Structured as a multi-page **Tableau Story** (`Overview Panel`) with compact multi-select dropdown filters for disease indicators, years, and continent groups.
* **Layout Optimization:** Standardized on a laptop-friendly resolution (Range: 1000x800 px to 1400x900 px) to ensure responsive rendering without unwanted scrollbars.
